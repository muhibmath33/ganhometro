# Interview script: people in Brazil paid from abroad

**For:** Xinning (in English) and Gabriel (in Portuguese, below). **Goal:** 10–15 conversations in the next 2–3 weeks, to check what [freelancer-market.md](../freelancer-market.md) concluded before we build sprints 4–8.

## What we want to learn

1. How they get paid: which services, PF or CNPJ, how often.
2. Whether they know how much is lost to fees and tax, and whether they care.
3. How they handle the carnê-leão or their accountant today, and what hurts.
4. Whether irregular income and missing CLT benefits (13º, férias, INSS) worry them, and what they do about it.
5. Whether they've had trouble proving income (renting, credit, visas).
6. What they'd pay for, and roughly how much.

**Rule:** ask about what they *did*, not what they *would* do. "Tell me about the last time…" beats "Would you use…?".

## Who to talk to

Aim for a mix. At least 4 should be individuals (PF) and at least 4 should have a company (CNPJ).

| Profile | How many |
|---|---|
| Developer or designer working full-time for one foreign company (PJ) | 4–5 |
| Freelancer with several foreign clients (direct or Upwork/Fiverr) | 4–5 |
| AI trainer (Outlier, DataAnnotation, Mercor…) | 2–3 |
| Online tutor (Cambly, Preply, italki) | 1–2 |

**Screening (all must be yes):** lives in Brazil · paid in foreign currency at least once in the last 3 months · handles their own money (not only a company's finance team).

## Where to find them

- **Communities:** r/brdev, "trabalhar para gringa" groups on LinkedIn, Telegram and Discord, and Brazilian Upwork/Fiverr groups.
- **Our waitlist:** anyone who answered "Freelancer" or picked an income range. Email the first 30.
- **Referrals:** at the end of each call, ask "Who else should I talk to?"
- **Thank-you:** offer free Pro for the first year at launch. It costs nothing now and filters for people who care.

## The call (25 minutes)

Ask permission to take notes (or record). Say there are no wrong answers and that we're testing our idea, not them.

### 1. Warm-up (2 min)
- What do you do, and for whom? How long have you been paid from abroad?

### 2. How the money arrives (5 min)
- Walk me through the last payment you received, from the client to the money in your Brazilian account.
- Which services do you use (Wise, Payoneer, Husky, PayPal, Remessa Online…)? Why that one?
- Do you know roughly how much you lost on that payment in fees and exchange? How did you find out?

### 3. Taxes and accountant (6 min)
- Do you receive as an individual (CPF) or through a company (CNPJ)? How did you decide?
- *If PF:* do you pay the carnê-leão every month? How do you calculate it? Which exchange rate do you use?
- *If CNPJ:* what does your accountant do for you, and what do you still do yourself? How much do you pay them?
- What was the most stressful moment you've had with the Receita or taxes?

### 4. Irregular income and safety net (4 min)
- How different is a good month from a bad one?
- Do you set money aside for taxes, 13º, vacation or INSS? How? Do you pay INSS?

### 5. Proving income (2 min)
- Have you ever had to prove your income: renting, a loan, a credit card, a visa? What happened?

### 6. Show the calculator (4 min)
- Share the screen: the "Quanto sobra" calculator on the site, with *their* numbers.
- Watch, don't explain. Ask: What surprises you? What's missing? What would you do with this?

### 7. Value (2 min)
- If an app did this every month with your real payments, and reminded you of the DARF, what would it be worth to you per month?
- Follow-up: at what price would it feel too expensive? At what price would it feel too cheap to trust?

### 8. Close (1 min)
- Can I add you to the waitlist (free Pro for a year)? Who else should I talk to?

## Roteiro em português (para o Gabriel)

Peça permissão para anotar. Diga que não existem respostas erradas: estamos testando a ideia, não a pessoa.

1. **Aquecimento:** O que você faz e para quem? Há quanto tempo recebe do exterior?
2. **Como o dinheiro chega:**
   - Me conta o último pagamento que você recebeu, do cliente até o dinheiro na sua conta.
   - Quais serviços você usa (Wise, Payoneer, Husky, PayPal, Remessa Online…)? Por quê?
   - Você sabe mais ou menos quanto perdeu nesse pagamento em taxas e câmbio?
3. **Impostos e contador:**
   - Você recebe como pessoa física (CPF) ou com CNPJ? Como decidiu?
   - *Se PF:* paga o carnê-leão todo mês? Como calcula? Que cotação usa?
   - *Se CNPJ:* o que o seu contador faz e o que você ainda faz sozinho? Quanto paga para ele?
   - Qual foi o momento mais tenso que você já passou com a Receita ou com impostos?
4. **Renda que varia e proteção:**
   - Qual a diferença entre um mês bom e um mês fraco?
   - Você separa dinheiro para imposto, 13º, férias ou INSS? Como? Paga INSS?
5. **Comprovar renda:** Já precisou comprovar renda (aluguel, empréstimo, cartão, visto)? Como foi?
6. **Mostrar a calculadora:**
   - Abra a calculadora "Quanto sobra" no site com os números da pessoa.
   - Não explique, observe. Pergunte: O que te surpreende? O que falta? O que você faria com isso?
7. **Valor:**
   - Se um app fizesse isso todo mês com os seus pagamentos reais, e lembrasse do DARF, quanto valeria para você por mês?
   - A partir de quanto ficaria caro demais? E barato demais para confiar?
8. **Fechamento:** Posso colocar você na lista (Pro grátis por um ano)? Quem mais eu deveria entrevistar?
