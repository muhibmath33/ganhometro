# Partnership and company checklist (Xinning + Gabriel)

**For:** Xinning and Gabriel. **Not legal or tax advice.** This lists what to settle in writing and what to ask a Brazilian accountant and lawyer **before the founding sale brings in money** (week 8).

## Why now

- **Revenue lands on Gabriel's CPF.** All customer payments go to Gabriel's personal account, so they count as Gabriel's personal taxable income (carnê-leão) until there's a company.
- **The project runs in Gabriel's name but is built by Xinning.** Xinning builds and runs everything from China, while the domain and the payment account are in Gabriel's name.
- **Both of you are exposed.** Without a written agreement, Gabriel carries the tax and legal exposure, and Xinning has no formal claim to the business he builds.

## 1. Agree in writing (a simple partnership agreement or memorandum)

1. **Roles and time:** Xinning handles product, development, marketing and sales. Gabriel handles the Brazilian-ID tasks (about 1–4 h/month).
2. **Ownership:**
   - who owns the code, the "Ganhômetro" brand, the content and the user data;
   - that the domain and the accounts in Gabriel's name are **held for the project** and move to the company once it exists.
3. **Money flow:**
   - Revenue arrives in Gabriel's account; expenses (marketer, ads, domain, accountant) are paid from it by Pix.
   - How and when profit is split and sent to Xinning.
   - Who pays which taxes.
4. **Costs before revenue:** who pays the first ~R$ 10.000 (handoff budget), and whether it's a loan, an investment or a cost share.
5. **Decisions:** what each of you can decide alone, and what needs both.
6. **Access:** Xinning gets his own logins to every account (Mercado Pago as a collaborator, registro.br contacts if possible). No shared passwords.
7. **Exit:** what happens if one of you wants to stop, the project is sold or it closes (domain, accounts, users, money).
8. **LGPD:** who is the **data controller** named in the privacy policy (Gabriel now, the company later?), and who answers user data requests.

## 2. Questions for a Brazilian accountant

1. **Tax on Gabriel's revenue:** until there's a company, how is revenue received by Gabriel as an individual taxed? (Carnê-leão on payments from individuals; does the 2026 R$ 5.000 exemption apply on top of his other income?)
2. **When to open the company:** at what monthly revenue is it worth opening a **CNPJ**, and which regime (Simples Nacional, which annex, Fator R) fits a software subscription sold to individuals in Brazil?
3. **Foreign partner:** can Xinning (living in China) be a partner (quotaholder) in a Brazilian LTDA? What does he need: a CPF for foreigners, a resident legal representative (Gabriel?), registration with the Banco Central?
4. **Sending money to Xinning:** what taxes apply (income tax withheld at source, IOF), and how much, in each case: as profit distribution to a foreign partner, as payment for services, or as royalties?
5. **Invoices:** must the business issue a nota fiscal (NFS-e) for each subscription? Can it be automated?
6. **Mercado Pago:** any limits or reporting for a CPF account receiving recurring sales? When must it switch to a CNPJ account?
7. **The carnê-leão estimate in the app and spreadsheet:** can the accountant review it before launch (the FAQ promises this)? See the five points in the README, section "Signup bonus".

## 3. Questions for a lawyer (optional at first, needed before the company)

1. A short partnership agreement covering section 1, valid while there's no company.
2. Whether "Ganhômetro" can be registered as a trademark at INPI (after the busca.inpi.gov.br search), and in whose name.
3. Terms of use and privacy policy review (the site has drafts at `/termos` and `/privacidade`).

## 4. Suggested order

1. **Now:** write and sign the simple agreement (section 1). Gabriel buys the domain.
2. **Before week 8:** accountant meeting (section 2); Gabriel opens Mercado Pago and adds Xinning as a collaborator.
3. **When revenue starts:** open the CNPJ at the point the accountant recommends, then move the domain, payment account and trademark to the company.
